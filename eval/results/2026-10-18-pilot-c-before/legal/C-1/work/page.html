What we collect

Your name, email, and address when you join; what you buy, so we can calculate your dividend; and rental records.

How we use it

To run your membership, process orders and rentals, and send the newsletter if you ask for it.

Who we share it with

Our payment processor and our shipping carrier, only as needed. We never sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Purchase records for seven years, as tax law requires; everything else until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Email privacy@larkspur.coop.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in, and no tracking cookies.
