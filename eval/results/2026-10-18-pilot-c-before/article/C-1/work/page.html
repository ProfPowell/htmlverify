Five knots every paddler should know

By Marta Ellison. Published 2 October 2026. 6-minute read.

You don't need fifty knots. You need five you can tie with cold hands.

Bowline

Makes a fixed loop that won't slip, and unties easily after a load.

Clove hitch

Ties a line to a post fast, and adjusts without untying.

Trucker's hitch

Tightens a load on a roof rack.

Figure-eight stopper

Keeps a line from running out through a cleat.

Taut-line hitch

Tensions tent guy lines.

Practice each knot ten times on the couch before you need it on the water.
