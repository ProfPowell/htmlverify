About Larkspur Supply Co.

Our story

Twelve paddlers and climbers founded the co-op in 1987 in a rented garage. Today 9,400 members own it, and every member gets one vote at the annual meeting.

What we believe

  1. Repair before replace. Our workshop fixed 3,100 items last year.
  2. Pay people fairly. Every employee earns at least the regional living wage.
  3. Leave places better. We give 2% of sales to trail and shoreline groups.

Milestones

  • 1987: Founded in a garage on Pier Road.
  • 1999: Opened the Alder Street store.
  • 2014: Started the rental program.
  • 2023: Opened the repair workshop.
