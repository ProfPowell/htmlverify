Larkspur Supply Co. opens the region's first gear library

Members can borrow tents, stoves, and snowshoes for free, starting 1 November 2026.

14 October 2026

Port Wren — Larkspur Supply Co. will open a gear library on 1 November 2026. Members can borrow up to three items for a week at no cost. The library starts with 240 items donated by members and repaired in the co-op's workshop.

"Most gear spends its life in a closet," said general manager Priya Raman. "A library puts it back on the trail."

The library will be open during store hours at 214 Alder Street.

About Larkspur Supply Co.

Founded in 1987, the cooperative is owned by 9,400 members and sells, rents, and repairs outdoor equipment.
